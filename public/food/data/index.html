Kitchen measures

Data

The figures behind these pages, free to download and reuse.

Two files, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: use and adapt them for non-commercial work, give credit, and share your changes the same way.

  • kitchen-measures.csv99 rows: one per container, food or yield, with low, middle and high values.
  • sources.csv23 sources, with what each supports and its caveat.

How to read it

Each row is one figure. "mid" is the best single value, and "low" and "high" are the range found. "plain_words" is the sentence used on the pages. Units are grams unless the unit column says otherwise. Rows flagged "No figure yet" have blank numbers. The files open in LibreOffice or Excel.

How sure are we?

  • National survey a weight printed in the national household survey's unit list.
  • Press report from a news article on market measures.
  • Study measured in a published study.
  • Textbook figure from a report or reference table, not measured on Nigerian food for this page.
  • Worked out calculated from other figures, with the assumption in the note.
  • Unchecked a rough figure we could not confirm.
  • Physical fact for example, 1 litre of water weighs 1 kg.
  • No figure yet nothing reliable found; the numbers are blank.

Everything here is desk research. None of it has been weighed by the author yet.
